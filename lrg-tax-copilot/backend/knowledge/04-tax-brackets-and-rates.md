## Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)
- 3.8% surtax on lesser of: net investment income OR MAGI exceeding threshold
- Thresholds: $200,000 (Single), $250,000 (MFJ), $125,000 (MFS)

## Self-Employment Tax
- 15.3% on first $176,100 of net SE earnings (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare)
- 2.9% Medicare on all net SE earnings above $176,100
- Additional 0.9% Medicare on SE earnings above $200,000 (Single) / $250,000 (MFJ)

## Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)
- Exemption: $88,100 (Single), $137,000 (MFJ)
- Phase-out begins: $626,350 (Single), $1,252,700 (MFJ)
- AMT rates: 26% on first $239,100; 28% on amounts above
