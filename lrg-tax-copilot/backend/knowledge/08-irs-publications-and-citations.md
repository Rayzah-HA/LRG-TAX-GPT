# IRS Publications & Citation Reference

## Key IRC Sections for Tax Preparers

### Income & Filing
- **IRC §1** — Tax imposed (rate tables)
- **IRC §61** — Gross income defined (all income from whatever source derived)
- **IRC §63** — Taxable income defined; standard deduction
- **IRC §67** — 2% floor on miscellaneous itemized deductions (suspended TCJA 2018-2025)
- **IRC §71** — Alimony (pre-2019 agreements: deductible; post-2018: not deductible)
- **IRC §86** — Social Security taxation thresholds
- **IRC §102** — Gifts and inheritances excluded from gross income
- **IRC §104** — Compensation for injuries or sickness exclusion

### Deductions
- **IRC §162** — Trade or business expenses (ordinary and necessary)
- **IRC §163** — Interest deductions (mortgage interest, investment interest)
- **IRC §164** — State and local tax deduction (SALT, capped at $10,000 under TCJA)
- **IRC §165** — Losses (casualty, theft)
- **IRC §167/168** — Depreciation (general / MACRS)
- **IRC §170** — Charitable contributions
- **IRC §179** — Election to expense certain depreciable business assets
- **IRC §199A** — Qualified Business Income deduction (20% QBI deduction)
- **IRC §213** — Medical and dental expenses (7.5% AGI floor)
- **IRC §280A** — Home office deduction; vacation home rules

### Credits
- **IRC §21** — Child and dependent care credit
- **IRC §22** — Credit for elderly and permanently disabled
- **IRC §24** — Child tax credit ($2,000 per qualifying child)
- **IRC §25A** — American Opportunity Credit / Lifetime Learning Credit
- **IRC §25D** — Residential energy efficient property credit
- **IRC §32** — Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)
- **IRC §36B** — Premium tax credit (ACA marketplace)
- **IRC §45** — Renewable electricity production credit
- **IRC §48** — Energy credit

### Penalties & Compliance
- **IRC §6651** — Failure to file / failure to pay penalties
- **IRC §6654** — Underpayment of estimated tax penalty (individuals)
- **IRC §6662** — Accuracy-related penalty (substantial authority standard)
- **IRC §6694** — Understatement by tax return preparer
- **IRC §7206** — Fraud and false statements

### Business & Entity
- **IRC §301** — Distributions of property (C-Corp)
- **IRC §351** — Transfer to controlled corporation
- **IRC §368** — Corporate reorganizations
- **IRC §704** — Partner's distributive share
- **IRC §1361-1379** — S Corporation rules
- **IRC §752** — Partner's share of liabilities

- **Welch v. Helvering** (1933) — Ordinary and necessary business expense standard
- **INDOPCO v. Commissioner** (1992) — Capitalization vs. deduction of expenses
- **Gregory v. Helvering** (1935) — Business purpose doctrine / substance over form
- **Frank Lyon Co. v. United States** (1978) — Economic substance doctrine
- **Cottage Savings Association v. Commissioner** (1991) — Realization of loss
- **Commissioner v. Duberstein** (1960) — Gift vs. income distinction

## Citation Format Guide

When citing tax authority, use these formats:
- **IRC Section:** IRC §162(a)(1)
- **Treasury Regulation:** Treas. Reg. §1.162-5(a)
- **Revenue Ruling:** Rev. Rul. 2023-14
- **Revenue Procedure:** Rev. Proc. 2023-34
- **IRS Notice:** Notice 2023-63
- **IRS Publication:** IRS Pub. 17
- **Court Case:** *Commissioner v. Glenshaw Glass Co.*, 348 U.S. 426 (1955)
