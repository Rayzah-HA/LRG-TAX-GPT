# One, Big, Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) — Key Tax Provisions

Reference: https://www.trumpaccounts.gov/

## Individuals

### Tax Inflation Adjustments (Multiple Sections)
The Act extends key TCJA-style parameters with updated inflation-adjusted amounts:
- **Standard deduction** — updated for TY 2025 & 2026 (MFJ, Single/MFS, HOH)
- **Individual marginal rate brackets** — 7-rate structure through 37% with 2026 threshold updates
- **AMT exemption amounts** and phaseout thresholds updated for 2026
- **Estate tax basic exclusion** — $15,000,000 for 2026
- **Adoption credit** — annual limit increased; refundable portion noted
- **Employer-provided childcare credit** — credit limit increased; higher limit for eligible small businesses

### Deduction for Seniors (Section 70103)
- **Effective:** 2025–2028
- Additional **$6,000 deduction** per eligible individual age 65+
- Up to **$12,000 MFJ** if both spouses qualify
- **Phased out** above specified MAGI thresholds

### No Tax on Tips (Section 70201)
- **Effective:** 2025–2028
- Above-the-line deduction for qualified tips (cash, charged, shared tips) in IRS-identified tipped occupations
- **Annual cap:** $25,000
- **MAGI phaseout** above threshold amounts
- **Exclusions:** Not available for tips connected to Specified Service Trades or Businesses (SSTBs)
- New reporting requirements for payors; IRS penalty/transition relief for 2025

### No Tax on Overtime (Section 70202)
- **Effective:** 2025–2028
- Above-the-line deduction for the overtime premium portion (the "half" in time-and-a-half)
- **Annual cap:** $12,500 (or $25,000 MFJ)
- **MAGI phaseout** above thresholds
- New reporting requirements for payors; transition relief for 2025

### No Tax on Car Loan Interest (Section 70203)
- **Effective:** 2025–2028
- Above-the-line deduction for interest on a qualified personal-use vehicle loan
- **Annual cap:** $10,000
- **MAGI phaseout** above thresholds
- **Loan requirements:**
  - Must originate after 12/31/2024
  - Must be secured by a lien
  - Must be for a qualifying vehicle
  - Leases do NOT qualify
- **Vehicle requirements:** Final assembly in the U.S.; VIN required on return when claiming
- Creates information reporting obligations for lenders/recipients

### HSA Expansion (Section 71307)
- **Telehealth safe harbor** made permanent — telehealth before deductible won't disqualify HSA eligibility (plan years beginning on/after 1/1/2025)
- Starting **1/1/2026:** Bronze and Catastrophic plans can be treated as HSA-compatible
- Starting **1/1/2026:** Certain direct primary care (DPC) arrangements won't disqualify HSA eligibility; HSA funds may be used tax-free for qualifying periodic DPC fees

## Families and Dependents

### Trump Accounts (Section 70204)
A new child savings account program:
- **No funding before 7/4/2026**
- One-time **$1,000 federal contribution** for each eligible child
- Annual contribution limits (including special employer contribution rules)
- **Investment restrictions:** Index-tracking funds only
- **Generally no withdrawals before age 18**, then IRA-like treatment
- Reference: https://www.trumpaccounts.gov/

### Adoption Credit Enhancement (Section 70402)
- Beginning for tax years after 12/31/2024
- Up to **$5,000 (indexed) of adoption credit may be refundable**
- Limits on how carryforwards affect refundability

## Businesses

### Vehicle Loan Interest Reporting — Transition Relief (2025)
- IRS transitional relief for 2025 reporting by lenders/recipients of qualified passenger vehicle loan interest (information returns + borrower statements)

### Employee Retention Credit (ERC) Limitation (Section 70605)
- Limits credits/refunds for ERC claims for **Q3/Q4 2021** that were filed after **1/31/2024**
- IRS FAQ guidance on timeliness and appeal rights

## Investment and Community Development

### Rural Opportunity Zones (Section 70421)
- Defines "rural area" (population/urbanized-area test)
- Reduces **"substantial improvement" threshold from 100% to 50%** for property located entirely in rural QOZs
- **Effective:** 7/4/2025

### Agricultural and Rural Lending Tax Benefit (Section 70435 / IRC §139L)
- Creates **IRC §139L**
- Eligible lenders may **exclude 25% of interest income** on qualifying loans secured by farm/rural real property
- Detailed qualification rules apply

## Clean Energy — Credit Expirations

### Clean Vehicle Credit Expirations (Sections 70501–70503)
Accelerated end dates:
- **§30D (New clean vehicle)** — not allowed after **9/30/2025**
- **§25E (Used clean vehicle)** — not allowed after **9/30/2025**
- **§45W (Commercial clean vehicle)** — not allowed after **9/30/2025**

### Home Energy Credit Expirations (Sections 70505–70507)
Accelerated end dates:
- **§25C (Energy efficient home improvement)** — not allowed for property placed in service after **12/31/2025**
- **§25D (Residential clean energy)** — not allowed for expenditures after **12/31/2025**

### Carbon Capture & Sequestration (Section 70522 / §45Q)
- OBBBA-related guidance/safe harbor approach for §45Q qualification/reporting for activities in calendar year 2025

## Tax-Exempt Entities and Charitable Giving

### Tribal Governments and Adoption Credit (Section 70403)
- Treats Indian tribal governments comparably to states for determining "special needs" for adoption credit eligibility

### Credit for Donations to Scholarship Organizations (Section 70411)
- **Beginning 1/1/2027**
- New **nonrefundable federal credit** for cash contributions to participating-state Scholarship Granting Organizations (SGOs)
- **Capped at $1,700/year** per individual taxpayer

## Other Taxes

### Dyed Fuel Claims (Section 70525 / §6435)
- Creates a claim (no interest) for previously paid excise tax on certain fuel later indelibly dyed and removed for nontaxable use
- Applies to eligible removals on/after **12/31/2025**

### Excise Tax on Remittance Transfers (Section 70604)
- **Beginning 1/1/2026**
- **1% excise tax** collected by remittance transfer providers on certain transactions paid with cash or similar physical instruments
- Deposit/filing obligations and limited penalty relief described by IRS
