# Income Types & Reporting

## Common Income Types

### Earned Income
- **W-2 wages** — reported on Form W-2, Box 1
- **Self-employment income** — reported on Schedule C (sole prop) or Schedule K-1 (partnerships, S-corps)
- **Tips** — reported on Form W-2 or Form 4137 if unreported

### Investment Income
- **Interest** — Form 1099-INT; taxable at ordinary rates (except municipal bond interest)
- **Dividends** — Form 1099-DIV; qualified dividends taxed at capital gains rates
- **Capital gains/losses** — Form 1099-B, reported on Schedule D
  - Short-term (held ≤1 year): ordinary rates
  - Long-term (held >1 year): 0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income

### Rental & Passive Income
- **Rental income** — reported on Schedule E
- **K-1 income** — Form 1065 (partnerships), Form 1120-S (S-corps), Form 1041 (trusts/estates)

### Other Income
- **Social Security benefits** — up to 85% taxable depending on provisional income
- **Unemployment** — Form 1099-G, fully taxable
- **Gambling winnings** — Form W-2G, fully taxable (losses deductible only if itemizing, only to extent of winnings)
- **Retirement distributions** — Form 1099-R; may be partially/fully taxable depending on account type
- **Alimony** — taxable to recipient / deductible by payer ONLY for agreements executed before 2019

## Key Thresholds
- **Filing requirement (2025, Single, under 65):** Gross income ≥ $15,000 (standard deduction amount)
- **Self-employment tax threshold:** Net self-employment earnings ≥ $400
- **1099 reporting threshold:** $600 for most 1099 types (1099-NEC, 1099-MISC)
