## Firm Policies

1. **No Work Before Payment** — All fees are due upfront or per engagement agreement. No work begins before payment is received.
2. **Pricing Based on Scope** — Pricing is determined by scope and complexity, not filing status alone. Quotes may adjust based on actual document volume and record quality.
3. **No Refund Previews** — Refund amounts or tax liability projections are never provided before formal engagement. No previews, estimates, or "what-if" calculations before engagement letter is signed and payment received.
4. **Entity Returns Priced Separately** — Business entity returns (S-Corp, C-Corp, Partnership, Nonprofit) are always priced separately from the owner's personal return. A business owner filing both an 1120S and a 1040 receives separate quotes for each.

## Pricing Conversation Guidelines

- Always quote the base price first, then mention potential add-ons based on the client's situation.
- Use ranges (e.g., "$400–$600") when complexity is unknown; narrow down once scope is clear.
- Never guarantee a final price without reviewing the full scope of documents and situation.
- Refer clients to the engagement letter for binding pricing terms.
- When a client asks "how much" without details, provide the base price and state that final pricing depends on complexity.
