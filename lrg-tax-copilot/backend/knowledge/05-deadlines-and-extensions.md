# Key Tax Deadlines & Extensions (2025 Tax Year)

## Individual Returns (Form 1040)
- **April 15, 2026** — Filing deadline (or next business day if falls on weekend/holiday)
- **October 15, 2026** — Extended deadline (Form 4868 must be filed by April 15)
- Extension gives extra TIME to file, NOT extra time to pay
- Penalties for late payment accrue from April 15 regardless of extension

## Estimated Tax Payments (Form 1040-ES)
- **Q1:** April 15, 2026
- **Q2:** June 15, 2026
- **Q3:** September 15, 2026
- **Q4:** January 15, 2027
- Safe harbor: Pay 100% of prior year tax OR 90% of current year tax (110% if prior year AGI > $150K)

## Business Returns
- **S-Corp (Form 1120-S):** March 15 (6-month extension to September 15)
- **Partnership (Form 1065):** March 15 (6-month extension to September 15)
- **C-Corp (Form 1120):** April 15 (6-month extension to October 15)
- **Nonprofit (Form 990):** May 15 (6-month extension to November 15)

## Other Important Deadlines
- **W-2s to employees:** January 31
- **1099-NEC to contractors:** January 31
- **1099-MISC:** February 28 (paper) / March 31 (electronic)
- **IRA/Roth IRA contributions for prior year:** April 15
- **SEP-IRA contributions:** Filing deadline including extensions
- **HSA contributions for prior year:** April 15
- **FBAR (FinCEN 114):** April 15 (automatic extension to October 15)

## Penalties
- **Failure to file:** 5% of unpaid tax per month, max 25%
- **Failure to pay:** 0.5% of unpaid tax per month, max 25%
- **Underpayment of estimated tax:** Based on federal short-term rate + 3%
- **Filing more than 60 days late:** Minimum penalty is lesser of $510 or 100% of tax owed
