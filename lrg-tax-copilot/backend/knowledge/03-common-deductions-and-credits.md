# Common Deductions & Credits

## Above-the-Line Deductions (Schedule 1)
- **Educator expenses** — up to $300 ($600 MFJ if both educators)
- **HSA contributions** — Self-only: $4,300; Family: $8,550 (2025)
- **Self-employment tax deduction** — 50% of SE tax
- **Self-employed health insurance** — 100% of premiums
- **IRA contributions** — up to $7,000 ($8,000 if 50+); deductibility depends on income and workplace plan
- **Student loan interest** — up to $2,500; phases out at MAGI $80K-$95K (Single), $165K-$195K (MFJ)
- **Alimony paid** — deductible only for pre-2019 agreements

## Itemized Deductions (Schedule A)
- **Medical expenses** — exceeding 7.5% of AGI
- **State and local taxes (SALT)** — capped at $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)
- **Mortgage interest** — on up to $750K of acquisition debt ($375K MFS)
- **Charitable contributions** — generally up to 60% of AGI for cash; 30% for capital gain property
- **Casualty/theft losses** — only for federally declared disasters

## Common Credits
- **Child Tax Credit** — $2,000 per qualifying child under 17; refundable up to $1,700
  - Phase-out: $200K (Single), $400K (MFJ)
- **Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)** — refundable; amount varies by income and number of qualifying children
  - Must have earned income; investment income must be ≤ $11,600
- **Child and Dependent Care Credit** — 20-35% of up to $3,000 expenses (1 dependent) or $6,000 (2+)
- **American Opportunity Credit (AOTC)** — up to $2,500/student for first 4 years; 40% refundable
  - Phase-out: MAGI $80K-$90K (Single), $160K-$180K (MFJ)
- **Lifetime Learning Credit** — up to $2,000/return; no limit on years
  - Phase-out: MAGI $80K-$90K (Single), $160K-$180K (MFJ)
- **Saver's Credit** — up to $1,000 ($2,000 MFJ) for retirement contributions; income limits apply
- **Premium Tax Credit (PTC)** — for marketplace health insurance; based on household income vs. FPL

## Business Deductions (Schedule C / Business Returns)
- **Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction** — up to 20% of qualified business income (IRC §199A)
  - Phase-out for specified service businesses: $191,950-$241,950 (Single), $383,900-$483,900 (MFJ)
- **Home office deduction** — Regular method (actual expenses) or Simplified ($5/sq ft, max 300 sq ft)
- **Business use of vehicle** — Standard mileage (70¢/mile for 2025) or actual expenses
- **Section 179 expensing** — up to $1,250,000 (2025); begins phasing out at $3,130,000 in total asset purchases
- **Bonus depreciation** — 40% for 2025 (stepping down from 100% in 2022)
