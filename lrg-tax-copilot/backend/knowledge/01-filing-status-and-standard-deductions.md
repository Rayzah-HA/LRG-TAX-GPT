# Filing Status & Standard Deductions (2025 Tax Year)

## Filing Statuses

1. **Single** - Unmarried, or legally separated/divorced on Dec 31
2. **Married Filing Jointly (MFJ)** - Married on Dec 31, both spouses report all income
3. **Married Filing Separately (MFS)** - Married on Dec 31, each spouse files own return
4. **Head of Household (HOH)** - Unmarried, paid >50% household costs, qualifying person lived with you >half the year
5. **Qualifying Surviving Spouse (QSS)** - Spouse died in prior 2 years, dependent child, paid >50% household costs

## Standard Deduction Amounts (2025)

| Filing Status | Standard Deduction |
|---|---|
| Single | $15,000 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $30,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $15,000 |
| Head of Household | $22,500 |
| Qualifying Surviving Spouse | $30,000 |

### Additional Standard Deduction (Age 65+ or Blind)
- Single / HOH: +$2,000 per qualifying condition
- MFJ / MFS / QSS: +$1,600 per qualifying condition per spouse

## Key Rules
- Cannot itemize AND take standard deduction — must choose one
- If MFS and one spouse itemizes, the other must also itemize
- Dependents: Standard deduction limited to greater of $1,350 or earned income + $450 (up to full standard deduction)
- Standard deduction is NOT available for nonresident aliens filing Form 1040-NR (generally)
